# Professional Growth Roadmap --- 90 Days

A practical 90-day plan to strengthen my full-stack engineering skills,
build credible portfolio evidence, and acquire my first independent
clients.

## Main objective

Build a clear, demonstrable professional profile that combines:

-   Full-stack web development
-   Business automation and AI-assisted workflows
-   Custom software development

The immediate commercial objective is to win the first paid client. The
longer-term target is to develop a repeatable client pipeline and work
toward an additional **USD 2,000--3,000 per month**. This is a goal, not
a guaranteed outcome.

## Starting point

I have around 10 years of web development experience. My current
self-assessment identifies React as a relative strength and several
areas to refresh or deepen, especially TypeScript, databases,
Git/GitHub, testing, deployment, and practical AI engineering.

This assessment is a starting hypothesis. We will validate it through
repository reviews, practical exercises, and finished projects---not
ratings alone.

## Professional positioning

**Working headline:** Full-Stack Developer \| AI-Assisted Software
Engineering \| Business Automation

The positioning will evolve as projects provide evidence. I will
describe skills and experience accurately and will not claim specialist
expertise that I have not yet demonstrated.

## The 90-day plan

### Phase 1 --- Foundation and diagnosis (Days 1--10)

-   Establish the professional profile, goals, and current skill
    baseline.
-   Review the development environment and AI-assisted workflow.
-   Audit existing projects and identify what can become credible
    portfolio evidence.
-   Research initial customer segments and choose a small, commercially
    useful portfolio project.
-   Set up a simple weekly execution and review process.

### Phase 2 --- Build demonstrable work (Days 11--30)

-   Complete one small, polished project with a clear business use case.
-   Practise UI/UX, TypeScript, frontend/backend integration, data
    modelling, and testing as required by that project.
-   Deploy a working demo and document setup, decisions, and
    limitations.
-   Resume a tightly scoped MVP demonstration of the Universal Assistant
    idea.
-   Avoid expanding projects before the core demo is useful and
    testable.

### Phase 3 --- Professional presence and sales readiness (Days 31--45)

-   Create or improve a bilingual personal website (Spanish and
    English).
-   Organize GitHub repositories and write concise case studies.
-   Define service packages, project boundaries, discovery questions,
    and proposal templates.
-   Practise explaining technical work in clear, client-friendly
    language.

### Phase 4 --- Market validation and prospecting (Days 46--65)

-   Test demand in the initial markets: Florida (especially the Latin
    business community), Colombia, and Mexico.
-   Identify relevant small businesses and independent professionals.
-   Send personalized outreach and track replies and conversations.
-   Conduct discovery calls and prepare scoped proposals based on real
    needs.

### Phase 5 --- First paid engagement and iteration (Days 66--90)

-   Work to close and deliver the first paid project.
-   Agree on scope, milestones, acceptance criteria, and communication
    before implementation.
-   Deliver, test, document, and request feedback or a testimonial where
    appropriate.
-   Review pricing, conversion, delivery effort, and the next client
    pipeline.

## Initial service areas

1.  **Web development:** landing pages, business websites, dashboards,
    and web application interfaces.
2.  **Business automation:** integrations, lead capture, workflow
    automation, and focused AI assistants.
3.  **Custom software:** APIs, portals, and SaaS features when the scope
    and business case are clear.

The initial emphasis is on web development and practical automation.
Larger custom-software engagements will be supported by stronger case
studies and delivery evidence.

## Project portfolio strategy

Existing projects may provide useful case studies, but unfinished work
must be presented honestly:

-   **Tourism SaaS:** an in-progress multi-tenant platform for tour
    operators. It can demonstrate architecture and product work, but it
    must not be presented as a finished product.
-   **Universal Assistant:** a potential AI and automation case study.
    Keep the MVP focused on answering inquiries, capturing leads,
    scheduling, and handing off to a human when needed.
-   **Trading bot:** a personal learning project. It is not an initial
    commercial offer or a priority portfolio item.
-   **Small completed projects:** build focused examples that solve
    recognizable business problems and can be demonstrated end to end.

## Learning principles

-   Learn in the context of projects rather than collecting
    technologies.
-   Use AI tools to accelerate work, but review, test, and understand
    the output.
-   Validate fundamentals with occasional exercises completed without AI
    assistance.
-   Prefer free or already-paid tools where practical; avoid adding
    subscriptions without a clear return.
-   Prioritize skills based on the next project and customer need.

## Weekly time budget

Target: **3--4 hours per day**, approximately 18--24 hours per week if
working six days.

A useful starting allocation is: - 30% focused learning and technical
review - 50% building and shipping - 20% evaluation, documentation, and
business development

We will adjust this allocation based on progress and client
opportunities.

## How we will work

1.  Work in small, clearly explained steps.
2.  Explain the purpose and expected result of each activity before
    asking me to do it.
3.  Review evidence before assigning the next task.
4.  Keep a visible backlog and record decisions.
5.  Do not assume a project, skill, or task is complete without checking
    it.
6.  Prefer finished, demonstrable outcomes over a long list of
    technologies.

## Success indicators

By Day 90, aim to have:

-   A clear bilingual professional profile and service description.
-   A polished personal website and organized GitHub presence.
-   At least one completed, deployed portfolio project and useful case
    studies.
-   A focused demo of the Universal Assistant MVP, if validation
    supports it.
-   A repeatable prospecting and follow-up process.
-   Real client conversations and proposals, with the objective of
    securing at least one paid engagement.

These are planning targets, not promises. We will review the metrics
regularly and adjust based on evidence.
